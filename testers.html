Join as a tester — 2 quick steps

First join our tester group, then install the app. The whole thing takes 2 minutes.

Step 2: Opt in on Google Play

You'll need a Google account and an Android phone. Stay opted in for 14 days — that's all we ask.

How to join — step by step

1

Join the Google Group

Open groups.google.com/g/testingdesiredgrowth and click Join group. This adds you to the tester list — Google requires this step before you can access the app.

2

Opt in on Google Play

Open the testing opt-in link on your Android phone. Tap Become a tester, then tap Download it on Google Play to install.

Note: If the opt-in page says you're not eligible, make sure you joined the Google Group first (step 1) using the same Google account.
3

Stay opted in for 14 days

That's it — you're in. You don't need to use the app every day. Just don't uninstall or leave the testing program for 14 days. Feel free to explore the app — choose a role (Dom or Sub) and try it out.

Important: If you leave the test before 14 days, it resets our counter. Please stay opted in even if you're not actively using the app.

Frequently asked questions

Why do you need 12 testers?
Google requires new apps to have at least 12 opted-in testers for 14 consecutive days before they can be published on Google Play. This is a policy for all new individual developer accounts — it's how Google verifies that apps are real and tested before going public.
Do I have to use the app every day?
No. You just need to stay opted in as a tester for 14 days. You're welcome to explore the app, but there's no usage requirement — just don't leave the testing program or uninstall during that period.
Is any data collected?
No. Desired Growth is fully offline. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics. Everything stays on your device. You can read the full privacy policy here.
What happens after 14 days?
After the testing period, we'll apply for production access on Google Play. Once approved, the app goes live for everyone. You can keep the app installed or uninstall it — totally up to you.
Is the app free?
Yes, Desired Growth is completely free. No in-app purchases, no subscriptions.
I have an iPhone — can I help?
The app is already live on the App Store for iPhone! For this testing round we specifically need Android users. But if you know anyone with an Android phone, please share this page with them.
