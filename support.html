Getting Started

  • Dom role (Android): Go to Settings, add a sub profile with their name and play types, then go to Tasks and tap Generate Tasks.
  • Sub role (iPhone): Tap the QR scan button on the Tasks screen and scan the code shown on the Dom's phone.
  • Switching roles: Tap the role selector on the home screen to switch between Dom and Sub views.

Common Questions

  • Does the app require internet? No — Desired Growth works completely offline.
  • Is my data backed up? Data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app will delete all data.
  • Why does it need camera access? Only to scan QR codes for task transfer. No photos are taken or stored.
  • Can I use it on one device? Yes — you can switch between Dom and Sub roles on a single device.
