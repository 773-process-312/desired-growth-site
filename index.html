About

Desired Growth is a private lifestyle management app for couples and individuals exploring Dom/sub dynamics. All data stays on your device — no accounts, no servers, no data collected.

Features

  • Weekly task generation tailored to individual preferences
  • QR code task transfer between devices
  • Hydration and fitness tracking
  • Built-in safety and consent resources
  • Fully offline — no internet required

Download

Available on iOS via TestFlight and Android via direct install. App Store release coming soon.
